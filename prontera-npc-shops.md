# Prontera NPC Shops

*Less time farming essentials, more time enjoying Midgard.*

## Why We Have NPC Shops

Return of Midgard is designed to let players spend less time repeatedly hunting for common items and more time enjoying Ragnarok Online.

Our Prontera NPC Shops offer a convenient way to prepare equipment, try different character builds, level, and participate in PvP and PvE activities. The goal is to make everyday preparation easier **without removing the value of monster hunting, quests, Mini-Boss encounters, or MVP battles**.

These shops offer **selected items only**. They are not intended to replace every item obtainable in the game.

## Available Shop Categories

The NPC Shops in Prontera are organized by item type:

| Shop Category | What You Can Find |
| --- | --- |
| **Supplies** | Selected consumables, potions, arrows, gemstones, and other useful supplies. |
| **Weapon Shops** | A selection of daggers, swords, spears, axes, maces, fists, staves, books, katars, instruments, whips, and weapons for Expanded classes. |
| **Armor & Equipment Shops** | Selected armor, shields, garments, footgear, and accessories. |
| **Card Shops** | Selected cards organized by their equipment slot: weapon, armor, shield, garment, shoes, headgear, and accessory. |
| **Headgear Shops** | Selected regular headgears obtainable from normal monster drops. |

**Please note:** The availability of a category does **not** mean every weapon, armor, card, headgear, or monster drop in that category is sold.

## Item Availability Rules

### Common Supplies and Equipment

The shops provide selected essentials so players can prepare more easily for leveling, different builds, and competitive activities. These items are meant to reduce repetitive farming for everyday preparation.

### Regular Monster Cards and Headgears

Selected cards and headgears obtainable from normal monsters may be purchased from the NPC Shops. **Not all normal monster drops are included.**

### Exclusive MVP and Mini-Boss Drops

**Items exclusive to MVP or Mini-Boss monsters are not sold in the NPC Shops.** These special rewards must still be obtained through hunting or their intended in-game acquisition methods.

### Items Shared With Normal Monsters

An item is **not automatically excluded** simply because an MVP or Mini-Boss can drop it.

If the same item can also be dropped by a **normal monster**, it may remain available in an NPC Shop **if it is included in the shop inventory**.

### Quest-Only Headgears

**Quest-only headgears are not sold in NPC Shops.** Headgears with no normal monster drop source must be obtained through their intended acquisition methods, including their respective quests where applicable.

## What Is Actually Available?

**The current NPC shop inventory is the final reference for what can be purchased.**

A normal monster dropping an item does not guarantee that the item is sold by an NPC. Only items included in the server's active shop list are purchasable. Shop inventory may be adjusted as the server is updated.

## Hunting and Quests Still Matter

Convenient access to common items does not make hunting unnecessary. Players still have reasons to explore maps, hunt for items not offered in shops, challenge Mini-Bosses and MVPs for exclusive rewards, and complete quests for special equipment and headgears.

**Our goal is simple: less repetitive preparation, more time enjoying the adventures, builds, and battles that make classic Ragnarok Online fun.**
