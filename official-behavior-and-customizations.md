# Official Behavior & Server Customizations

Return of Midgard takes a **strict, conservative approach to gameplay customization**. Our goal is to preserve the familiar behavior and feel of **classic Pre-Renewal Ragnarok Online**, particularly the pRO-era, rather than redesigning the game around custom mechanics.

We prioritize classic skill and combat behavior, classes, quests, monsters, MVP encounters, and Episode 12 progression. Changes are considered carefully; the purpose of server-specific features is mainly to improve access, preparation, and convenience **without unnecessarily changing the core game**.

**Important:** This does **not** mean Return of Midgard is an unchanged official server. The server has documented rules, rates, and convenience features that differ from a traditional official setup.

## Core Gameplay We Aim to Preserve

- **Pre-Renewal mechanics and class progression** — classic first- and second-job classes, Transcendent classes, and supported Expanded classes; no Third Jobs.
- **Episode 12 game world and data** — maps, monster drops, and content are based on the intended Episode 12 era, with documented exceptions.
- **Classic skills and combat** — avoid unnecessary skill redesigns and changes made only for a more modern playstyle.
- **MVP hunting and quest-based rewards** — MVP-exclusive rewards are earned through hunting, while official quest headgears still require their corresponding quests.
- **Classic pRO-inspired interface** — preserve the familiar old-school client atmosphere instead of replacing it with a modern UI.

This is our **design direction**, not a claim that every detail is identical to every historical official Ragnarok server. Bug fixes and explicitly documented exceptions may still apply.

## Important Exceptions & Boundaries

- **MVP-exclusive drops are not sold in NPC shops.** If an item is also obtainable from a regular monster, it may still appear in a shop; items exclusive to MVPs remain hunting rewards.
- **Official quest headgears are not sold in NPC shops.** For example, Mr. Smile, Sunday Hat, and Boy's Cap remain quest rewards.
- **Episode scope:** Most regular content follows Episode 12 and earlier. Selected headgears, Cash Shop items, and Cash Box items may extend to Episode 13, as described in the Episode 12 guide.
- **Gameplay features and server operations:** PvP, WoE schedules, rankings, events, and optional services are documented separately and may have server-specific rules.

## Our Customization Policy

When considering a new feature or adjustment, we ask: **Does this preserve the classic experience, or unnecessarily change how Ragnarok plays?**

We prefer to **keep official-style gameplay behavior** and clearly document the differences that are necessary for this server. Convenience should make it easier to enjoy classic Ragnarok—not turn it into a different game.
