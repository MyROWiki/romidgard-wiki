# Gym Pass — Ripped Cabus

**Ripped Cabus** is a carrying-capacity training NPC in Prontera. Training with him increases your character's item carrying capacity.

![Ripped Cabus](images/gympass_npc_sprte.gif)

## Location

| Name | Map | Coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ripped Cabus | Prontera | `150,280` |

Ripped Cabus can be found in **Prontera at `150,280`**.

![Ripped Cabus location in Prontera](images/gympass_map.jpg)

## Required Items

| Item | Preview | Where to Get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gym Membership Card** | ![](images/gym_membership_card.bmp) | Cash Merchant |

Bring **1 Gym Membership Card** each time you want to increase your carrying-capacity training level.

Each successful training session consumes **1 Gym Membership Card**.

## How It Works

1. Purchase a **Gym Membership Card** from the Cash Merchant.
2. Talk to **Ripped Cabus** in Prontera at `150,280`.
3. Choose **Yes** when he asks if you are ready to train.
4. If you have a **Gym Membership Card**, he will consume one card and increase your carrying-capacity training by one level.
5. Repeat the process if you want to increase it further.

Your carrying-capacity training can be increased a maximum of **10 times**.

| Training Status | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 0–9 previous sessions | You may train again with 1 Gym Membership Card |
| 10 completed sessions | Maximum carrying-capacity training reached |
| No Gym Membership Card | Training cannot continue |

## Maximum Training Limit

Ripped Cabus allows a character to increase this carrying-capacity training up to **10 times**.

After reaching the tenth training level, he will no longer accept additional carrying-capacity training for that character.

## Restoring Previous Training

The dialogue option **“Um, my workouts wore off.”** is used to restore your previously recorded carrying-capacity training level if the skill is no longer active.

If the character already has prior training recorded, Ripped Cabus will reapply that same training level. This recovery option does **not** increase the level beyond what you previously earned.

If the character has never trained with Ripped Cabus before, there is no previous training level to restore.
