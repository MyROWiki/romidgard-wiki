# Differences from Other Servers

Return of Midgard preserves a classic Ragnarok Online interface inspired by the old Philippine Ragnarok Online (pRO) experience. The screenshots below compare our **Old UI** with examples of the **New UI** found in newer Ragnarok clients or other servers. Interfaces may vary between servers.
