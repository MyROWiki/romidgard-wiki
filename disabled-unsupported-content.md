# Disabled & Unsupported Content

## Costume Items

**Costume items and costume equipment are not available in Return of Midgard.** Regular headgears and equipment remain available through their usual in-game sources, but they are not costume items. This keeps the server focused on the classic Pre-Renewal equipment experience.

## Auto-Attack / Auto-Farming

**Not available and not planned.** Return of Midgard does not provide an automated auto-attack, auto-play, or auto-farming feature. Players play and control their characters through the classic Ragnarok Online combat system; ordinary basic attacks and skills remain available.

## Battlegrounds

**Battlegrounds is an Episode 12 feature, but it is currently disabled on the server.**

Battlegrounds queues, maps, NPC access, and related rewards are not currently available. If the feature is enabled in the future, this page will be updated with the appropriate gameplay and access information.
