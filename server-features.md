# Server Features

Return of Midgard combines classic Pre-Renewal gameplay with competitive and convenience systems designed for the 99/70 Episode 12 era.

## Classic Pre-Renewal

- Maximum Base / Job Level: 99/70
- Maximum Stat: 99
- Maximum ASPD: 190
- Instant Cast: 150 DEX
- Base EXP / Job EXP Rate: x1 / x1
- Non-MVP monster drops: 90%
- MVP default drops: x1

## Permanently Free-to-Play & Ready for Action

The server is **permanently free-to-play** and is designed to help players prepare quickly for **PvP, War of Emperium, and MVP hunting**.

Players receive **free Zeny** that can be used in the in-game shops to purchase:

- Armor and equipment
- Cards and Headgears
- Potions and consumables
- Supplies and other essentials

**Shop exceptions:** Equipment, cards, and headgears that drop from regular monsters are sold in NPC shops, but **MVP drops** are not. **Official quest headgears** such as Mr. Smile, Sunday Hat, and Boy's Cap must be obtained through their respective quests. See [Item Availability & NPC Shops](items-and-npc-shops.md).

A **Job Changer NPC** and **Miracle Tonic** are also available so players can build and prepare characters faster.

The purpose of these features is to reduce unnecessary preparation time while keeping the actual PvP, WoE, MVP, and classic gameplay experience at the center of the server.

Premium is optional and is not required to access the core game.

## Episode 12 World

The playable world is limited to content appropriate for **Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc** and earlier. Third Jobs and later-era progression are not part of the normal server world.

Most regular items and monster drop tables follow Episode 12 data. Selected headgears, Cash Shop items, and Cash Box items may include content up to Episode 13. See [Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc](episode-12-the-demise-of-morroc.md) for the full item, drop, and map scope.

The user interface and selected client features follow the style many players remember from the old Philippine Ragnarok Online era, helping the game feel immediately familiar while keeping the server focused on classic gameplay.

## MVP Hunting & PK

Classic MVP hunting is a major part of progression and competition. **PK is enabled on MVP maps**, so players may face other adventurers while competing for MVP monsters and their drops.

The server also includes MVP ranking activities for additional competitive goals.

See [MVP & PK Maps](mvp-pk-maps.md) for the current Episode 12 MVP-map list.

## PvP

Players can test builds, equipment, and teamwork through supported PvP environments and ranking systems.

## War of Emperium

### Current Schedule

War of Emperium is currently scheduled **every day from 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM (GMT+8)**.

The daily schedule gives players and guilds more opportunities to participate in WoE, test builds and teamwork, and compete for castles. The schedule may be adjusted later based on community activity, guild participation, and overall server population.
