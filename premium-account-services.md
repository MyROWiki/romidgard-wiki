# Premium Account Services

Premium is an optional account-wide service that provides additional convenience and progression benefits while preserving the server's classic gameplay direction.

## Premium Benefits

While Premium is active, the account receives:

- **+50% EXP Rate**
- **+50% Drop Rate**
- **50% Reduced Death Penalty**
- **Increased Kafra Storage**
- **Access to the Dungeon Teleport NPC and Agit Maps**
- **Change Name Service** — a **Ticket of Identification is still required** when using this service.
- **Repair Service**

## Required Items

| Item | Preview | Where to Get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Premium Box** | ![](images/item_premium_box.png) | Cash Merchant |

To activate Premium, obtain a **Premium Box** and use the item on your account. The account becomes Premium after the box is used successfully.

Premium Boxes can be purchased from the **Cash Merchant in Prontera** and from Cash Merchants located in other towns.

A complete list of **Cash Merchant locations and coordinates** will be added to this wiki later.

> **Change Name Service:** A **Ticket of Identification** is also required when using this specific Premium service. Its preview and acquisition details will be added once documented.

## Premium Account Notification

When an account has active Premium status, a Premium notification is shown on screen:

- When logging in
- Whenever changing maps

If the account is **not Premium**, this Premium notification is not shown.

![Premium Account Notification](images/premium-account-notification.png)
