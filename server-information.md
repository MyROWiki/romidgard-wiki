# Server Information

Return of Midgard is a **classic Pre-Renewal Ragnarok Online server** built around the 99/70 era, Episode 12 progression, MVP hunting, PvP, and guild competition.

The server is **permanently free-to-play**, with optional Premium services available for additional convenience.

## Official Behavior & Customization Policy

Return of Midgard follows a **strict approach to customization**. We aim to preserve classic Pre-Renewal gameplay and official-style behavior, avoiding unnecessary changes to core mechanics. Where we intentionally adjust the server—such as **non-MVP drop rates, free Zeny, NPC shops, and convenience services**—we document those differences openly.

See [Official Behavior & Server Customizations](official-behavior-and-customizations.md) for the full policy and customization list.

## Platform Support

**Return of Midgard is available for PC only.** Mobile devices, including Android phones and iPhones, are **not supported**. There is no official Android or iOS game client.

For installation instructions, see [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

## Drop Settings

Normal non-MVP monster drops are set to **90%**.

**MVP default drops remain at x1** and are not changed to the 90% non-MVP rate.

### Rates Under Review

The rates currently published in this Wiki are **provisional where noted and subject to change** as we review the server's final drop settings.

- **MVP Drop Rate: x1 — Confirmed.**
- **Mini-Boss Drop Rate: Under Review.**
- **Normal Monster Drop Rate: Under Review** (currently listed as 90%).

Until an official change is announced, the rates already published in this Wiki remain the reference for the current server settings. Any finalized adjustments will be announced and documented here.

## Free-to-Play Access

The server is designed so players can prepare for competitive and end-game activities without needing to purchase basic equipment or supplies with real money.

Players receive **free Zeny** that can be spent in the in-game shops on:

- Armor and equipment
- Cards and Headgears
- Potions and consumables
- Supplies and other essentials

A **Job Changer NPC** and **Miracle Tonic** are also available to help characters get ready faster for **PvP, War of Emperium, and MVP hunting**.

Premium remains optional and is not required to access the core game.

## Battlegrounds Status

Battlegrounds belongs to the **Episode 12 era**, but the feature is **currently disabled on the server**.

Battlegrounds queues, maps, NPC access, and related rewards are not currently available. The wiki will be updated if the feature is enabled later.

## Class Progression

The server follows the classic job progression of Ragnarok Online.

### Regular and Transcendent Classes

Players can begin as a Novice, advance through the classic first- and second-job paths, then rebirth and continue into their Transcendent class path.

### Classic Expanded Classes

The following Expanded class paths are available:

- Super Novice
- Taekwon Kid
- Soul Linker
- Taekwon Master
- Ninja
- Gunslinger

Third Jobs and later-era advanced class evolutions are not part of the server's Episode 12 progression.

## Gameplay Focus

The server is designed around the classic activities that defined the 99/70 era:

- Character leveling and job progression
- Party play
- Equipment and card farming
- MVP hunting
- Player versus Player
- War of Emperium
- Guild competition
- Classic economy and item progression

## MVP Map PK

PK is enabled on MVP maps. Players hunting MVP monsters should be prepared for competition from other players in addition to the MVP encounter itself.

See the full [MVP & PK Maps](mvp-pk-maps.md) list for the current Episode 12 configuration.

## Episode Scope

The playable world is limited to content appropriate for **Episode 12 and earlier**. Later-episode maps, Third Jobs, and later-generation progression are outside the intended server scope.

For item, monster-drop, map, and database scope details, see [Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc](episode-12-the-demise-of-morroc.md).
