# Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc

Return of Midgard is centered on **Episode 12: The Demise of Morroc**, the classic Pre-Renewal era before Third Jobs and later-generation progression.

## Episode Scope

The normal game world includes content appropriate for **Episode 12 and earlier**. Character progression remains focused on:

- Classic first- and second-job classes
- Transcendent classes
- Classic Expanded classes
- Pre-Renewal mechanics
- Classic equipment and card progression
- MVP hunting, PvP, and War of Emperium

Third Jobs and later-era advanced class evolutions are not part of the server's intended progression.

## Battlegrounds Status

**Battlegrounds is part of the Episode 12 era, but it is currently disabled on the server.**

Battlegrounds queues, maps, NPC access, and related rewards are therefore not currently available to players.

This does not change the server's Episode 12 content direction; it simply reflects the current server configuration. If Battlegrounds is enabled in the future, this wiki will be updated with the appropriate access and gameplay information.

## Item Availability

Most regular items available in the game are limited to **Episode 12 and earlier**.

There are selected exceptions for convenience and cosmetic content. Some **headgears, Cash Shop items, and Cash Box items** may include content released up to **Episode 13**.

These exceptions do not change the server's main Episode 12 progression, maps, classes, or monster-drop direction.

## Monster Drops & Database Information

Monster drop tables follow **Episode 12 data**.

Because of this, drop information found on third-party databases such as **RateMyServer.net** may not always match the server. External websites may display data from a different episode, server version, or later database revision.

The server plans to add an in-game **`/database`** feature so players can check the actual monster drops directly in-game.

Monster spawn locations have not been custom-relocated simply to match third-party databases. The server follows its intended Episode 12 world and map setup.

## The Demise of Morroc

Episode 12 brings the Morroc region into one of the most memorable chapters of classic Ragnarok Online. The destruction surrounding Morroc and the threat of **Satan Morroc** define the era and introduce high-level content tied to the aftermath of the disaster.

For the current PK-enabled MVP map list, see [MVP & PK Maps](mvp-pk-maps.md).

## Why Episode 12?

The goal is to preserve the classic 99/70 Ragnarok experience while still offering a broad world for leveling, farming, MVP hunting, guild competition, PvP, and exploration.

Keeping the world within Episode 12 also prevents later classes, maps, equipment, and systems from changing the intended classic balance.
