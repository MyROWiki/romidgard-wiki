# Job Changer

*Spend less time on job-change preparation and more time enjoying your adventure in Midgard.*

The **Job Changer** allows eligible characters to change into supported classes or begin rebirth directly through an NPC. This convenience service makes it easier to explore different character builds while preserving the class progression and level requirements of Return of Midgard's Pre-Renewal setting.

## NPC Location

| NPC | Location | Job Change Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Job Changer** (Valkyrie) | **Prontera `146,105`** | **Free (0 Zeny)** |

Find the Valkyrie-style **Job Changer** NPC in Prontera and speak to her to begin.

## How to Change Jobs

1. Reach the required Job Level or Base Level for your next class.
2. Spend all unused **Skill Points**.
3. Remove any **cart, falcon, or mount** (including cash mounts).
4. Speak to **Job Changer** at Prontera `146,105`.
5. Select **Change Job**, choose an available class, and confirm your selection.

The NPC checks your character's eligibility and displays a message if a requirement has not yet been met.

## Job Change Requirements

| Progression | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Novice → First Class** | **Job Level 10** |
| **First Class → Second Class** | **Job Level 40** |
| **Novice → Super Novice** | **Base Level 45** and eligible Novice job progression |
| **Second Class → Rebirth** | **Base Level 99** and **Job Level 50** |

**Job changes are free.** There is no Zeny fee for changing jobs through this NPC.

### First Classes and Expanded Options

An eligible regular **Novice** can select from the six classic first classes:

- Swordman, Mage, Archer, Acolyte, Merchant, and Thief

The NPC also offers supported Expanded paths from a regular Novice: **Taekwon Kid, Super Novice, Ninja, and Gunslinger**, subject to their requirements.

A **Taekwon Kid** at the required Job Level may advance to either **Soul Linker** or **Star Gladiator** (Taekwon Master).

Supported **Baby Novice** class choices are also available to characters already on the Baby class path.

### Second Class Progression

At **Job Level 40**, an eligible first-class character can choose from the second-class options available to that class. The Job Changer follows the character's class path rather than providing an unrestricted list of jobs.

## Rebirth and Transcendent Classes

Eligible regular second-class characters may begin **Rebirth** once they reach **Base Level 99 and Job Level 50**.

The Job Changer changes an eligible character into a **High Novice** and resets their levels for the rebirth journey. The NPC is configured to **retain the previous class path** so the character can continue through the corresponding Transcendent progression.

Rebirth is not an option for every Expanded class.

## Important Rules and Restrictions

- **Use all Skill Points first.** The NPC does not allow job changes while unused Skill Points remain.
- **Remove carts, falcons, and mounts.** These must be removed before the change can proceed.
- **Level requirements still apply.** The NPC does not skip the minimum Base Level or Job Level requirements listed above.
- **Class restrictions still apply.** Super Novice, Ninja, and Gunslinger have no further job advancement through this NPC in the current Pre-Renewal setup.
- **No Third Jobs.** Later class progressions outside Return of Midgard's Pre-Renewal scope are not supported.

### Platinum Skills

The Job Changer is configured to **automatically grant applicable Platinum (quest) skills** after a successful job change. Availability depends on the character's class.

## Why We Offer a Job Changer

Return of Midgard aims to reduce repetitive preparation so players can spend more time **experimenting with builds, leveling, joining parties, taking on MVPs, and participating in PvP and War of Emperium**.

The Job Changer offers convenient class progression, but players must still meet the necessary levels and follow the supported class paths.
